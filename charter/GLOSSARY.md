## Statistics policy

PRIMARY FAMILY (new ledger row X32). There are seven headline claims: C1, C2-SOUR, C3, C7, C8, C9 and C12, so m = 7 [Aitzaz Q4, 24 Oct]. Holm step-down at a familywise alpha of 0.05: sort the seven single p-values from smallest to largest and compare them in turn with 0.05/7 = 0.00714, 0.05/6 = 0.00833, 0.05/5 = 0.0100, 0.05/4 = 0.0125, 0.05/3 = 0.0167, 0.05/2 = 0.0250 and 0.05/1 = 0.0500. Reject in that order and stop at the first p that is above its level; that claim and every later one fail. If Aitzaz rules that taste never takes part in recognition, C2-SOUR leaves the family before the Wave 1 freeze and m = 6, giving levels 0.00833 to 0.05 [Aitzaz Q2, 24 Oct]. C4 may join only if its N is raised to 100 before the Wave 1 freeze, which makes m = 8 [Aitzaz Q4, 24 Oct]. Family membership and m are fixed at the Wave 1 freeze (X02), including C9 as a committed member even though C9 itself is frozen in Wave 2; Wave 2 may not add or remove members. If C2-SOUR leaves (m = 6) or C4 joins (m = 8), that also happens before the Wave 1 freeze. No document may hard-code a level such as '0.005' or 'at most 10 primaries'; every document cites X32. PLACEHOLDER-79 is closed by X32.

ONE RULE FOR EACH PRIMARY'S SINGLE p. A claim's Holm p is the largest one-sided p among its named effect parts. This is an intersection-union rule: because every effect part must pass, the false-positive rate cannot rise. The direction of every effect part is pre-registered. A part written as a confidence bound is converted into a one-sided p against its margin (non-inferiority or superiority). Any bound printed for an effect part is taken at the Holm level at which the claim is finally tested, never at a fixed 95%. The named effect parts are:
- C1: (2) D_beh(O) minus D_beh(Z), a one-sided permutation test stratified by family; and (4) FarMinusNeutral, a one-sided Wilcoxon. (4) loses its separate fixed 0.01.
- C2-SOUR: non-inferiority p-values for (1) mean Delta_far above -0.10, (2) Delta_far(SOUR) minus Delta_far(SWEET-CONTROL) above -0.10, and (3) mean Delta_look below +0.10.
- C3: the stratified log-rank one-sided p for E50, Frankie versus B1n-mind.
- C7: T7(1), T8(c) versus NO-CHANGE, and T8(c) versus HUNGER-YOKED.
- C8: S1; S6 seed versus dud; S6 seed versus the reference cell; S7 versus NO-SEED; S7 versus YOKED-FRUIT.
- C9: C9-M1, the one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on G.
- C12: one-sided Mann-Whitney tests of R23 versus B0 and of R23 versus B1n-seq. Step 2 (R3) is a fixed-sequence follow-on: it is tested only if step 1 passes, at the level C12 received in Holm, with no extra alpha.

OTHER PARTS INSIDE A PRIMARY.
- Explanation gates are each tested at one-sided 0.05, uncorrected. Examples: C7's BDC, FIXED-RANDOMNESS and NO-RICH-ARM gates and T8(b1)/(b2); C8's S1b, S2, S3, S4 and S6b; C9-M2 to C9-M6; C12's hit-rate and switched-off comparisons; C3 (4). Gates decide what a pass is credited to or how it is labelled, never whether the effect exists.
- Floors are fixed thresholds on estimates (ratios, shares, minimum differences). They have no test and must hold.
- Validity, applicability and precondition checks are pass/void conditions. Where one needs a test, it uses one-sided 0.05.

ONE ALPHA FOR SECONDARY EVIDENCE. One secondary family of four members, Holm-corrected among themselves at a familywise 0.05 (levels 0.0125, 0.0167, 0.025, 0.05). Each member forms its single p with the same largest-p rule:
- C4: effect parts (a) versus no-objection, (b) versus no-gating, (d) e_in versus the flat tower, and (d) e_out versus gates forced to maximum.
- C6: effect parts (1) versus empirical chance, and (2) to (4), each under both responder and intention-to-treat scoring.
- C2-MIRROR: effect parts Delta_look below 0, and Delta_far non-inferiority at -0.15. A_look_post at most 0.30 and the MIRROR-CONTROL difference at most +0.10 are floors.
- C13-E (E1): effect parts KEPT versus REMOVED (Wilcoxon) and versus B1n-mind (Mann-Whitney).
This replaces C6's 0.01 and 0.05, MIRROR's 0.01 and E1's 0.005. Secondary results go in a separate table and are never merged into the headline tally. The optional T15 keeps its own Holm across its 5 controls at 0.05, outside both families.

MECHANISM CHECKS. C5, C10, C11, C13-M, C2-MEMORY, C4 (c), C8-S8 and every T0 check use one-sided 0.05 per test, unless the claim pre-registers a stricter level (C5 keeps its 0.01). A level is never looser than that, and these results are never counted as evidence. Two-sided tests are used only where no direction is pre-registered: differences from a twin, and 'not significantly below 0' checks. Crediting to a conventional twin uses each claim's stated 90% equivalence interval and sits outside every family. The cautious-start replicate is reported outside every family and is never a pass condition. PLACEHOLDER-80 is replaced: there is no two-sided default and no rank-biserial floor.

PART-LEVEL TAGS. In the charter table every part carries exactly one tag: PRIMARY-EFFECT, SECONDARY-EFFECT, GATE, FLOOR, VALIDITY, APPLICABILITY, MECHANISM or REPORTED. The claim-level tag is read off its effect parts. The mixed claims are tagged as follows:
- C2: SOUR (1) to (3) PRIMARY-EFFECT; SOUR (4) and the per-half means FLOOR; (5) APPLICABILITY; (6) REPORTED; (7) VALIDITY. MIRROR is SECONDARY-EFFECT, with the 30-room-Frankie rule as APPLICABILITY. MEMORY is MECHANISM.
- C4: (a), (b) and (d) SECONDARY-EFFECT; (c) MECHANISM; the bot profile VALIDITY.
- C8: S1, S6 and S7 PRIMARY-EFFECT; S1b, S2, S3, S4 and S6b GATE; S5 FLOOR; S8 MECHANISM; S9 VALIDITY.
- C13 is split into C13-M (M1 to M7, MECHANISM) and C13-E (E1, SECONDARY-EFFECT).
- C12: step 1 PRIMARY-EFFECT; step 2 PRIMARY-EFFECT (fixed sequence); H and switched-off GATE; rate-ratio 2 FLOOR; T15 REPORTED (optional, own family).
- Twin crediting: in every claim (C1, C2, C3, C4, C6, C7, C9, C10, C12, C13-E) the twin-crediting part is tagged GATE and sits outside every family. It uses the claim's 90% equivalence interval and stated margin; a two-sided difference p, where computed, is REPORTED only.
A claim counts in the evidence tally only through its PRIMARY-EFFECT or SECONDARY-EFFECT parts. N comes from the joint-power rule (X39), may only go up, and is never changed after results are seen.

## Shared definitions

**Document map.** claims-perception = C1-C6. claims-drive = C7, C8, C12, C13 (C13-M and C13-E). claims-social = C9, C10, C11. ledger = the ledger rows (W, B, M, T, E, X, I). register-forbidden = the register of unspecified details (PLACEHOLDER rows) plus the forbidden list. Every routing instruction uses this map.

**ID conventions and claim-qualified part names.** Tests are written T0-T15 with no leading zero. Ledger teacher rows are T01-T18. Baseline ids (B0, B1t, B1n-*, B2-B9) are not ledger body rows (B01-B21). Gamer bots are G01-G18, off-switch versions OS01-OS23 (OS22 retired), conventional twins TW1-TW7 (and TW1u), test fixtures MK1-MK10, and prior-control cohorts PC1-PC3. A part of a claim is always named with its claim, because C9 and C13 both have parts M1-M6/M7 that clash with ledger rows M01-M53: so C9-M1, C13-M3, C13-E1, C8-S7, C7-T8(c). The pending-question tags are written exactly [Aitzaz Q1, 24 Oct] to [Aitzaz Q5, 24 Oct].

**Decision and principle numbering.** D1-D16 means only the 24 Oct 2026 decision list, cited in the ledger's decision_ref column. As tagged there: D1 pre-registration, freezes, sealed vaults and closure library; D2 two-way essence; D3 write rule; D4 untried value and newborn start; D5 energy-dependent randomness and insurance; D6 satisficing; D7 temperament and fair comparison; D8 sharp-look spending and evolved perception; D9 social learning with no copy urge; D10 glimmer stone, place memory and seeds; D11 plan elimination; D12 spiking neurons; D13 outside-in tower; D14 novelty, wiggle and experience certificates; D15 who lives in a world; D16 taste and no nibble. The v1.1 proposal's principles_kept 'decision 1-8' are renamed P1-P8 everywhere: P1 no LLM inside; P2 a less-optimal path is worth more, and conventional plumbing only; P3 hybrid mind; P4 evolution shapes recognition only; P5 randomness proposes and character decides; P6 curiosity as insurance; P7 teacher as scaffolding; P8 patience follows teacher reliability. Every 'decision N' in any document becomes DN or PN depending on which list it means. Bare 'decision N' is not allowed.

**Holm-adjusted level.** The level each primary claim is tested at, under ledger X32 (m = 7; strictest step 0.05/7, about 0.0071). Secondary evidence uses its own family of 4 at a familywise 0.05. The full rule is in the statistics policy. The literal values '0.005' and '10 primaries' are retired.

**Part-level tag.** Each part of a claim carries one tag: PRIMARY-EFFECT, SECONDARY-EFFECT, GATE, FLOOR, VALIDITY, APPLICABILITY, MECHANISM or REPORTED. C13 is split into C13-M (mechanism check) and C13-E (secondary evidence). The evidence tally counts only parts tagged as effect parts.

**B1n family.** Four separately named items replace the bare name 'B1n'. B1n-bot is the standalone eat/skip probe bot, used in C1, C2 and C4. B1n-forager is B1n-bot's memory on Frankie's body with greedy movement, used in C7. B1n-mind is Frankie with nearest-neighbour memory in place of the tower and write rule, used in C3, C13-E and the X29 gate. B1n-seq is the sequence keep-and-retry learner on Frankie's menu, used in C12 for T13 and T15. Ledger X36 holds all four. Every citation names its variant.

**T1 groups O, Z, Z0.** O (100) eats one super-sweet h1. Z (100) eats one never-seen nourishing kind U from an unrelated family, matched to h1 as follows: U's energy gain within plus or minus 1 unit of h1's, and each of U's 4 taste numbers (PLACEHOLDER-47) within plus or minus 0.05 of h1's, that is 5% of that taste number's full range (Claude placeholder, PLACEHOLDER-101), on the same host and in the same scene. Z0 (50, reported only) has no taste. The headline comparison is O versus Z. C2's H2-ONLY clones come from group Z, so they have already eaten U.

**Ledger test row (X10-X26).** A short pointer to the claim that owns the test, holding only the frozen numbers copied from that claim: X10-X11 to C1, X12 to C2, X13 to C3, X14 to C4, X15 to C5, X16 to C6, X17-X18 to C7, X19 to C8, X20 to C9, X21 to C10 (a mechanism check), X22 to C11, X23 and X25 to C12, X24 to C13, and X26 to the statistics policy and X32. The claim text holds the rule; the row never restates it differently.

**Energy bands.** well-below: E <= R-10. near: R-10 < E < R+20. well-above: E >= R+20. These are C7's bands, frozen in the Stage 4 specs as new ledger row M54. They are the only bands used anywhere: C7, C13, habit similarity (PLACEHOLDER-98) and log field energy_band.

**Satisficing bar and boldness.** The bar is the predicted chance of staying above R over the drive horizon that an option must reach. One rule: boldness lowers the bar, with required chance = 0.9 - 0.3 x b (PLACEHOLDER-27) [Aitzaz Q3, 24 Oct]. PLACEHOLDER-22 is restated as 'the bar comes from PLACEHOLDER-27'. Boldness does not reweight losses. C13 and C7's boldness split cite this rule and no other role.

**Option order and no-safe-option fallback.** The habit option is considered first, then proposals in the order drawn (PLACEHOLDER-23) [Aitzaz Q3, 24 Oct]. If no option clears the bar, Frankie takes the option with the best chance of staying above R (ledger M47) [Aitzaz Q3, 24 Oct]. That choice is tagged action_provenance=no-safe-option-fallback with no_safe_option=true, and its share is reported (C7). This is the only place a best-score rule is allowed inside Frankie. The backups-don't-count gate covers it.

**Decision tick (satisficing chooser).** A sampled tick for C13 is one of two types. Type close-call: an option ahead of the chosen one in the chooser's order missed the bar by less than delta, or the chosen option cleared the bar by less than delta. On a no-safe-option tick, where the fallback is a best-score rule, close-call means another option is within delta of the fallback's chance. Type choice-change: the choice differs from the previous tick's. delta = 0.05 in predicted chance of staying above R (PLACEHOLDER-99). Margins come from the replayed drive trace (M53). PLACEHOLDER-67 ('top two options within 0.05') is retired.

**Proposal-count curve, N_max and fractional counts.** Above and near R, PLACEHOLDER-25 is unchanged: 1 proposal within 10 units of R, rising in a straight line to 4 at R+40. Below R-10 the count rises at that Frankie's own desperation slope s (PLACEHOLDER-26): count = 1 + s x (R-10-E), capped at N_max = 6 (PLACEHOLDER-97). The FIXED-RANDOMNESS value is the curve at R+20, which is 2 under the placeholder, and is unaffected. A fractional count x is rounded stochastically: floor(x)+1 with probability equal to the fractional part, drawn from the proposal stream (PLACEHOLDER-96). So a T14 redraw also redraws the rounding.

**Hysteresis.** There is none in the main version (PLACEHOLDER-25, PLACEHOLDER-100). The hysteresis-off control (OS22) and the 'hysteresis state' log field are removed from X08 and C13. If Aitzaz defines hysteresis before Stage 6, it gets a ledger row and C13's conditional pass rules come back.

**Habit similarity and habit contribution.** The similarity score counts how many of three parts match between a situation and a habit origin: scene, top recognised kind in view, and energy band (M54). It runs from 0 to 3. 'Similar' means 3/3, and C13's 'window threshold' is 3 (PLACEHOLDER-98, which replaces PLACEHOLDER-29). Habit contribution is 'zero' when no habit weight entered the choice. C13-M3 and C13-E1 use exactly these terms.

**Struck option; reasoned and rote.** A struck option is an option the chooser ruled out by prediction before trying it. It is logged as a struck-option event in struck_options and never as an action or a provenance value. 'Eliminated-by-prediction' is retired as a provenance value. The strike class is decided on the world side, the same way for C3, C7 and C12 and for every agent. It is rote if this agent's ledger shows at least one own eat of, or sickness from, any instance of the same world kind before the strike. It is reasoned only if that kind has zero own eats. It is no-kind if the option has no object kind (a route move), and no-kind strikes never count as reasoned. Watched exposures of the kind are logged alongside the strike. The noise-width version of PLACEHOLDER-38 is retired, and ledger M31 is restated this way. Tower elimination among candidates is a different thing and keeps the name elimination_events.

**Top-up and yoking interval.** 50 ticks. TOPPED-UP: every 50 ticks after tick 5,000, E is raised back to its tick-5,000 level if it is below it. Yoking: at every 50-tick checkpoint the difference from the partner is removed or added evenly over the next 50 ticks. Both are logged injection_events (W45) and printed on the priors card. PLACEHOLDER-83 (100 ticks) is retired.

**Designed-exposure policy.** Only these tests may force an eat, and only at these points. T1: h1 for O and U for Z, if not eaten by choice within the 200-tick choice window. T2: h2, the SWEET-CONTROL apple, and the MIRROR and MIRROR-CONTROL look-alikes, if not eaten within the 30-tick window. T4: F and P_F learning-phase eats, after 3,000 ticks without them. T5: one designed eat of a nothing-nature instance per look-alike kind. T6: each of the 3 known kinds once, if needed. T8: the pre-exposed half's designed walk past B at tick 3,000 with exactly one taste. No other test may force an eat; T7, T9, T10 and T13 are named explicitly, and adding a test needs a register change before the freeze. Every designed exposure is flagged (action_provenance=designed-exposure), printed on the priors card, and the chosen and forced subgroups are reported separately. Setting energy at a probe is an energy injection, not an exposure. This rewrites PLACEHOLDER-85. Kill test (KILL_TEST.md, bench only): forced eats are allowed only for the 40 warm-up tastes and the 8 teaching tastes, and for h2 and SWEET-CONTROL if skipped (PLACEHOLDER-85).

**Forced eat with no verdict.** A designed exposure with no committed verdict, or with verdict 'unknown', is treated as an eat judged 'unknown'. A new candidate is created as in PLACEHOLDER-09, with no blame step, and logged with memory_write_events trigger=designed-exposure (PLACEHOLDER-93, for Aitzaz to initial). C1, C2, C4, C5 and C6 cite this one rule.

**Stomach delay in test lives.** Every protocol eat uses d = 10 whatever the life's age. The applied value is logged in eat_events.stomach_delay_d.

**Visit and patient harvest.** This is C8's definition, shared with C10. A visit to a spot is a run of consecutive ticks in which the agent is within 4 cells of the spot or has its sharp view on it. Runs separated by fewer than 20 ticks merge into one visit. Discovery is the first visit to the real seed. Patient harvest means all four of these: discovered the real seed before its first fruit; left it uneaten until it became a tree; made at least 1 visit per 100 ticks between discovery and first fruit; ate at least one fruit from that tree. Dud-waiting (C10) means 2 or more visits to the dud over the same period. C10's 300-tick harvest window and its old 3-cell visit definition become reported only.

**T13 group and freeze.** The primary T13 group is 100 evolved, teacher-free Frankies, frozen in Wave 1 (Stage 4) with T0-T9 and T14. **Survival-floor policy in T13 rooms.** There is no survival floor. Energy is never reset when it reaches 0, and dying at 0 (B06) holds in every room and every pre-room life. Each problem room is a separate clone from the agent's room-entry snapshot at the matched life age, so a death in one room does not affect the others. Entry energy is set once at entry by a logged energy-set injection (Frankie's cohort median reserve + 10 for agents with no reserve). This set never depends on low energy. Reaching room-entry age is an entry condition applied the same way to every agent type: each type's room cohort is the first 100 lives, in pre-registered seed order, that reach room age, and deaths before room age are reported per type. Inside a room, death ends that room. The denominator for R3 and R23 is always the scheduled 2,000 ticks per room, so ticks after death count with zero eats; dying is never rewarded. Death events and own-log length are covariates, and a per-ticks-lived version is reported. Ledger W46 is rewritten to say this.

**Value table (boundary of the forbidden entry).** Allowed: tallies (counts or sums of energy changes) of Frankie's OWN outcomes for a situation or option. Each tally is updated only from outcomes seen within a fixed credit window after that choice, with no discounting, and is never updated from another estimate. This covers M34's habit weights (logged as habit_weight_updates), PLACEHOLDER-21's counting estimator, and the trust and influence updates of PLACEHOLDER-40 and PLACEHOLDER-41, whose credit window is 300 ticks after the watched event (Claude placeholder). Banned: anything that updates one situation's value from another situation's value or estimate, including TD errors, Bellman backups, discount factors, Q-tables, value functions and eligibility traces that pass value backward across situations. Review checks the habit and drive design notes against this line. Aitzaz confirms it at initialling.

**Boredom counter (boundary of the forbidden entry).** Forbidden: any quantity, per object, per kind, per place or cell, or global, that grows or shrinks with the number of looks, visits or exposures whatever their outcome. This includes a per-place look count for an object that never moves. Allowed: fading place-and-time records, each tied to an observed outcome ('investigated here, nothing came of it'), and these records may add up [Aitzaz Q2, 24 Oct]. T0(e) code check: run scripted repeated looks with the outcome-record write intercepted. Any mind state that still changes with the number of looks, apart from the sensory buffer, flags a counter. PLACEHOLDER-109 holds the rule.
